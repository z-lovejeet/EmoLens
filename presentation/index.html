The Inspiration

"What’s wrong? How are you feeling?"

50%
of autistic individuals experience Alexithymia — the inability to recognize and name internal emotional states.
"My sister teaches at an autism school in India. I watched kids freeze when asked how they felt. Not because they didn’t care, but because their brain couldn't connect what their body felt to a word."

The Flaw in Existing Tools

Every wellness app demands the exact skill these students lack: pick an emoji, choose from a mood wheel, rate from 1 to 10.

Faces and facial icons introduce emotional ambiguity and anxiety instead of clarity.
"How do you feel?"
"Where do you feel it?"

The body already knows the answer. A tight chest. A racing heart. Heaviness in the stomach. We don't ask for emotion words — we ask for physical sensations.

Chest Tightness Buzzing Hands Heat in Face Heavy Stomach 3D Interactive Somatic Map
Somatic Truth

"Every body has a story.
We just need to learn how to read it."

Built so that the next time a child in my sister’s classroom is overwhelmed, they don’t have to suffer in silence.
